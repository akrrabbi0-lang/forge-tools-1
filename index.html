What's on Forge

Forge is a small collection of everyday tools that run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or upload leaves your device unless you specifically choose to share it (like generating a QR code for a memo). No sign-up needed for most tools, no ads slowing things down while you work.

Word Counter

Paste or type text to see word count, character count, sentence count, paragraph count, and estimated reading time — updates as you type.

QR Code

Generate: turn any link or text into a scannable QR code, choose a size, download as PNG. Scan: use your camera or upload a photo of a QR code to read what it contains.

Password Generator

Create a strong, random password. Choose the length and which character types to include (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols), then copy it.

Unit Converter

Convert between common units of length, weight, and temperature. Pick a category, enter a value, and see the converted result instantly.

Image Resizer

Shrink an image's dimensions or file size right in your browser. Upload a photo, set a max width and quality, then download the resized version.

Audio Converter

Pull the audio track out of a video file and save it as a WAV file. Upload a video, click convert, preview the result, then download.

Background Remover

Upload a photo and click anywhere on its background to remove it. Works best on plain, fairly solid-colored backgrounds. Adjust the tolerance and edge softness sliders to fine-tune the cut.

Memo

Write short text notes that stay saved on this device. Optionally share any memo with a QR code — scanning it (with Forge's own Scan tool) on the same device or account opens that memo.
